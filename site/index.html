Pocketbuff GitHub

Freebuff, in your pocket.

Chat with the Freebuff on your computer from your phone. Approve edits, watch diffs, pick up terminal chats. Private over your own Tailscale network, and free.

Paste this into Freebuff

Read https://raw.githubusercontent.com/SIDDHANTCOOKIE/freebuff-remote-control/main/site/install.md and follow it

Or in a terminal: curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/SIDDHANTCOOKIE/freebuff-remote-control/main/site/install.sh | bash -s -- --yes

  1. Freebuff sets it up

    Installs everything it needs, starts Pocketbuff in the background and puts it on your tailnet.

  2. You sign in to Tailscale once

    The only step that needs you. Freebuff gives you the link.

  3. Open the link on your phone

    It pairs with a one-time code. Add it to your home screen.

PrivateOnly devices on your tailnet can reach it.
FreeUses your Freebuff login. No API key.
